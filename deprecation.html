Hello dear user! This serves as a notice that the "Dynamic Fog of War" extension has been deprecated, and will stop working on the 1st of January, 2027.

Since its creation, Owlbear Rodeo has evolved immensely, as has its ecosystem, and there are now several alternatives to this extension that are much faster and with many more features. Those are:

  • Smoke & Specter - Smoke & Specter is the evolution of this extension; it started from the same baseline, but has since evolved into much more than Dynamoc Fog of War could ever be; it includes a way to convert your maps from Dynamic Fog of War.

  • Dynamic Fog - This is an official OBR extension to provide the same kind of functionality, with a smaller set of features, easier to get a hold of.

Please switch to an alternative as soon as possible, and thank you for using DFOW! Click here to return to the main page.
